How it works

Requisition to shortlist, without a recruiter in the room

Every stage runs on its own. A person steps in only at the final decision — or whenever they choose to.

1

The job goes live

Write a brief; Auralis drafts the description, flags exclusionary language, and turns the requirements into a scoring rubric. It posts to your careers page and syndicates to the boards you use.

2

Every resume gets read properly

Not keyword matching. Each application is parsed, enriched, and scored against the rubric and your own hiring bar — with the evidence for each score recorded.

3

Candidates book themselves in

Those who pass get an invitation and choose a slot in their own timezone. No calendar ping-pong, no recruiter coordination, no days lost.

4

The interview actually happens

At the booked time, a live voice-and-video interview runs round by round. It asks follow-ups based on what the candidate just said, opens a whiteboard or code editor when the question calls for it, and proctors throughout.

5

Scores arrive with their evidence

Each competency is scored against your rubric and cites the exact moment that earned it — playable, timestamped, quoted. Pass the bar and the next round schedules itself.

6

Everyone hears back

Including the people you decline. Specific, honest, written feedback — the thing almost no company has the hours to do, and the thing candidates remember.

Why it's different

Built for the part everyone else skips

No score without its evidence

Every number links to the moment that produced it — the audio, the transcript line, the code that was written. A hiring manager can check the reasoning in seconds instead of trusting it.

Compliance is the feature

Bias audits, candidate notice, human-review paths, and full audit trails are built in — not bolted on. NYC LL144, Illinois AIVIA, and the EU AI Act are handled as configuration, not consulting.

Signal the take-home lost

Overlay tools broke unproctored assessments. A live, adaptive conversation with follow-ups the candidate can't pre-compute is the only screen that still measures anything — and we record how work was built, not just what was submitted.
